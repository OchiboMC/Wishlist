# Wishlist

Wishlist helps you track the items you want to craft and the materials you need in Minecraft.

## Requirements

- Java 17
- Minecraft 1.20.1
- Forge 47.4.x or Fabric Loader 0.16.14 with Fabric API
- JEI and EMI are optional; use a version for Minecraft 1.20.1 and your mod loader

The mod only needs to be installed on the client.

## Installation

Place the Wishlist mod file for your loader in your Minecraft `mods` folder.

## Controls

- Press `O` to open Wishlist.
- Press `H` while a container is open to highlight required items.
- Hover an item in an inventory, container, JEI, or EMI and press `T` to add it to Wishlist. `Shift+T` adds one to the active Wishlist immediately.
- Use the star button on a recipe to add it to a wishlist.
- Search your Wishlist by item name or ID, such as `stick`, `minecraft:stick`, or `create`. Matching items keep their full material trees visible.

The Wishlist screen can open its settings from the title in the top-left corner. The settings include HUD visibility, size, item count and background opacity.

Use recipe expansion to view the materials needed for an item. JEI or EMI is required when choosing between multiple recipes for an ingredient.
